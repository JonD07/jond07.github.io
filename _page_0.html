Bio

Hello, my name is Jonathan Diller!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Robotics at Colorado School of Mines. I am a member of the Pervasive Computing Systems (PeCS) research group, working under Dr. Qi Han. I completed a master's degree in Computer Science at Colorado School of Mines in 2022 and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science with minors in Mathematics and Mechatronics Technologies at Penn State Harrisburg. Prior to starting postsecondary education, I served in the United States Marine Corps for five years.

In my free time I enjoy skiing, golfing, hiking, and playing video games. I'm also an avid traveler and love to explore new places and try new foods.
